Kingdom Crush – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Kingdom Crush is an offline puzzle game. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

  • No account or sign-in. The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Local progress only. Your level, coins and castle decorations are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party tracking.
  • No in-app purchases. Coins and boosters are earned by playing.
  • Internet is used only to load a text font; no data is sent.
